These terms apply to entering and running the Midvaal MadMac, organised by Meyerton Athletics Club (MAC). Online entries are taken through Race Pass, whose own terms apply to the payment and the entry transaction itself — these terms cover the race.

1. Entry fees and refunds

The club still has to decide this.

Whether entry fees are refundable, and under what circumstances. Most South African road races say entry fees are non-refundable; the club needs to say so explicitly, or say what the exception is.

2. Transfers and substitutions

The club still has to decide this.

Whether an entry can be moved to another runner or another distance, by when, and at what cost. Race Pass may already impose its own rules here — check them first so the two do not contradict each other.

3. If the race is cancelled or postponed

The club still has to decide this.

What happens to entries if the race is called off or stopped part-way — weather, a municipal permit withdrawal, a safety incident. This is the clause that gets read when it matters and the one most often missing.

4. Minimum ages

The club still has to decide this.

ASA rules set minimum ages by distance, and they change. Confirm the current figures with ASA or AVT rather than copying last year's flyer.

5. Your health, and your own risk

The club still has to decide this.

The wording entrants agree to when they enter. This should come from the club's insurer or ASA's standard indemnity, not be drafted here.

6. Photography and images

The club still has to decide this.

Photographs of runners are already published on this site's gallery page. Under POPIA that is processing of personal information and needs a stated basis, plus a route for someone to ask for a photo of themselves to be taken down.

7. Entering

Online entries close on 1 October 2026 at 21:00. Manual entries available at collection points only (not on race day), subject to availability. No entries on race day.

8. Licences and race numbers

ASA-affiliated runners must wear their licence number front and back. Non-affiliated runners need a temporary licence at additional cost and must run in neutral or white colours — required for the 42.2km, 22km and 11km, not the 5km.

9. Rules of the race

Run under WA, ASA and AVT rules. No seconding, no dogs, no pets, no headphones (unless required by organisers), no cyclists.

Mat-to-mat chip timing with checkpoints on route. Distance boards: every 1km. Waterpoints: roughly every 3km.

10. Age categories and prizes

Age categories determined as at date of competition; proof of age and ID required at prize giving.

11. Appeals

Referee's decision final; appeals require a form and a R500 deposit, refunded if successful.

12. Your personal information

How the club handles your personal information — including your results, which are published on finishtime.co.za — is set out in our Privacy Policy.

13. Changes to these terms

We'll update the date at the top of this page if anything here changes materially. Questions about any of it go to midvaalmadmac@gmail.com.
