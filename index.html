Qualifying for 2027

Built for the sub-5:00 chase

MadMac exists in the calendar exactly where the two biggest qualifier races need it to. Here's what actually matters if you're chasing a marathon qualifying time.

1

Single lap

Single lap, no repeated loops — the marathon does not repeat a circuit, though the course does include out-and-back sections.

2

Sub-5:00 standard

42.2km in under 4:59:59 (sub-5:00) qualifies for both the Comrades centenary and Two Oceans 2027. MadMac's own cut-off is generous — see the note below.

3

Chip-timed and GPX-verified

Mat-to-mat chip timing with checkpoints on route. The marathon measures 42.24km against the official GPX — within normal route-drawing tolerance of the standard 42.195km marathon distance.

4

Early in the window

2027 qualifying opens 15 June 2026 – 3 May 2027 for Comrades and qualifiers run after 1 May 2026 for Two Oceans. MadMac falls near the start of both — get it done in October and you've still got months in hand if the day doesn't go your way.

6h30 is the MadMac cut-off. 42.2km in under 4:59:59 (sub-5:00) is the qualifying standard. The race cut-off is 6h30. The qualifying standard for both Comrades and Two Oceans is 4:59:59 — finishing inside the cut-off does not mean you have qualified.

Why run this one

Four reasons, no adjectives

01

One lap, not three

A lot of qualifier marathons in Gauteng are two or three laps of a short circuit, and runners actively dislike them. MadMac's marathon runs single lap — out to Karee Road, across the R59 into the Randvaal smallholdings, and home through Daleside village, with no repeated loop.

02

R100 000, paid equally

Men's and women's prize money is equal across every distance and every age category — not just the open field. Paid electronically, by EFT, within seven days.

03

Four distances, one morning

42.2km, 22km, 11km and 5km, all starting and finishing at Café du Cirque. A club or a family can enter one event together, from age 9 up.

04

Five collection points, not one

Number collection runs at Sportsmans Warehouse branches in Cresta, East Rand Mall, Fourways and Vanderbijlpark before race week, plus the venue itself — no drive to Meyerton required before race day.

The route

Four routes, one start line

The marathon rolls gently to halfway, climbs once around 20km, then drops away over the final two kilometres into the finish. The 22km front-loads its climbing early and descends home. The 11km stays closest to the village, in the suburban streets short of the smallholding grid the 22km reaches. The 5km barely leaves the flat — 13m of vertical spread across the whole loop.

Comrades edition — Centenary Down Run

MadMac's online entries close 22 September 2026 at 21:00 — the same day the Comrades centenary ballot closes. Two qualifier-shaped decisions, one week, one date.

Comrades Marathon 2027 — the centenary

The 100th edition — Centenary Down Run, run 13 June 2027. Qualifying window: 15 June 2026 – 3 May 2027, standard: a 42.2km marathon under 4:59:59.

Totalsports Two Oceans Marathon 2027

The 56km Ultra, run 4 April 2027. Qualifiers run qualifiers run after 1 May 2026 count, and a marathon qualifying time is also used for seeding at two oceans.

Is MadMac fast enough for that standard?

Yes: about 240–320m of total ascent across 42.24km, computed from the official GPX — the same territory as a fast big-city marathon. The first 20km rolls gently between 1 470m and 1 520m, with the low point around 16km. The only meaningful climb comes between 20km and 24km, gaining about 70m, steepest in the two kilometres to 24km. From 24km to 40km it's rolling at 1 510–1 550m, then it drops away over the final two kilometres into the finish. The downhill finish is a real asset if you're chasing a time.

What "single lap" actually means here

The marathon does not repeat a circuit — the thing runners actually object to in multi-lap qualifier races. It does include out-and-back sections, visible on the map above; "single lap" means no repeated loop, not that the course never doubles back on itself.

  • The standard: 42.2km in under 4:59:59 (sub-5:00), for both Comrades and Two Oceans seeding.
  • MadMac's cut-off: 6h30 — generous, but not the same number as the qualifying standard.
  • Altitude: the course sits at roughly 1500m. Most of the field is Gauteng-based and acclimatised; if you're travelling from the coast to chase a qualifier, budget for that.

MadMac's qualifier positioning for 2026/2027 was covered by Vaalweekblad and The Citizen in August 2026, alongside other Vaal-region marathons offering early centenary qualifying opportunities.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is MadMac an approved Comrades and Two Oceans qualifier?

MadMac is positioned and marketed as a single-lap Comrades and Totalsports Two Oceans Marathon qualifier, and has carried that status in previous editions. Written confirmation of the 2027 approved-qualifier listing for both races is being obtained — check back closer to race day if this matters to your entry decision.

What time do I need to run to qualify?

Under 4:59:59 for the 42.2km, for both the Comrades centenary and Two Oceans 2027. That's the qualifying standard, not MadMac's own 6h30 cut-off — see the qualifier panel above.

Can I enter on race day?

No. Manual entries are available at the collection points listed above, subject to availability, but there is no entry on race day itself.

Can I cancel my entry?

No, cancellations aren't allowed. This is a Race Pass entry-terms policy, not a MadMac-specific one — see Race Pass's own terms for the full detail.

Can I substitute my entry to another person?

Yes, substitutions are allowed up to 20 days before race day, from your Race Pass "My races" page.

Can I transfer my entry to another race?

Yes, transfers to another race are allowed up to 20 days before race day, from your Race Pass "My races" page.

Where do I collect my race number?

Five points across Johannesburg and the Vaal in race week, plus the venue itself — see the number collection table above. A Postnet-to-Postnet delivery option is also available through Race Pass.

How will I find my race number?

Race Pass publishes a race-number lookup closer to race day, linked from your entry confirmation.
